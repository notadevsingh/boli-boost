Privacy Policy for Boli Boost

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 • Package ID: dev.soraatelier.boliboost • Developer: Sora Atelier
🛡️ 100% Private No personal information or account required.
🎙️ Local Audio Only Voice is analyzed ephemerally and never saved or sent to servers.
🚫 Zero Ad Tracking No Advertising ID (AAID) collection or behavioral profiling.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Boli Boost, an educational French language-learning application crafted for children, parents, and language enthusiasts. Sora Atelier ("we", "our", or "us") is deeply committed to protecting the privacy of young learners and complying strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Boli Boost adheres to a strict "no collection" policy. We do not gather, store, or transmit any personal data:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not access device location (neither precise nor approximate).
  • We do not access user files, photo libraries, or device contacts.
  • We do not collect or transmit the Android Advertising ID (AAID). The AD_ID permission has been explicitly removed from our application manifest.
  • We do not incorporate third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral monitoring SDKs.

3. Microphone Usage & Audio Processing

Boli Boost includes interactive speaking exercises where children can practice pronunciation for letters, words, and numbers.

  • Purpose: The microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is used exclusively to evaluate speech pronunciation accuracy during active exercises.
  • Ephemeral & Local: Spoken audio is processed in real time in temporary device memory via Android's local speech recognition service (android.speech.RecognitionService).
  • No Server Uploads: Audio recordings are never stored on disk, never uploaded to any remote server, and never shared with third parties.
  • Immediate Deletion: The audio buffer is immediately released from memory as soon as the pronunciation score is calculated.
  • Manual Fallback: If microphone access is declined or unavailable, learners can still progress through all activities using manual confirmation buttons.

4. Local Progress Storage

All learning statistics—such as unlocked curriculum levels, quiz scores, and daily practice streaks—are stored locally on the user's device using standard web storage (localStorage). This information never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any cloud database.

5. Advertising & Monetization

  • Current Release: This release of Boli Boost is completely ad-free. There are no commercial advertisements displayed.
  • Future Updates: If child-friendly advertisements are ever introduced in future updates, they will strictly comply with Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ad Network requirements, will disable personalization (npa: true), and will mandate child-directed flags (tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true) across all ad sessions.

6. Children's Privacy Compliance

Our application is designed to provide a safe, secure, and educational environment for children:

  • We do not require account creation, registration, or logins.
  • We do not condition participation in any learning exercise on the provision of personal data.
  • Parents can clear all locally saved data at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store personal data on remote servers, there is no remote data to retain or delete. To remove all local data, cached lessons, and audio permissions, simply clear the app storage in your Android device settings:

Settings > Apps > Boli Boost > Storage > Clear Data
